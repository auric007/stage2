# stage2(operational class rules)
### Patterns of errors
- Of 2,041 missed objects, 1,123 got a box with the wrong class. That class is almost always a physical neighbour: ICV ↔ tank ↔ vehicle, missile kit ↔ truck, SPA ↔ tank, logistic or smallvehicle ↔ vehicle, camo ↔ what's under it.
- **The right answer is usually the second-highest score.** On ICVs called `vehicle`, tank is second 55% of the time (median 0.69). On missiles called `vehicle`, missile is second 54% of the time. That's why score-based rules work.
