What this says:

- **2 m doesn't work as an object-only test.** With objects floating on grey, the model loses its calibration and calls 305 vehicles "radar" and 217 "camo". Phase 1's OBJECT variant answers the object-only question properly.
- **With 10 m of surroundings, most class decisions are already close to full.** Average F1 goes 53 → 60 from 10 m to the full tile: +4 from 30 to 100 m and +3 beyond 100 m.
- **Radar** improves up to 30 m, mostly through precision (85% → 93%), then stops. Its pad and immediate surroundings matter; the wider site doesn't.
- **Missile depends most on surroundings.** F1 is 37–39 up to 30 m and 46 at 100 m, with precision rising from 55% to 67–73%. That fits launchers being recognised partly from the layout of their site.
- **Logistic** improves gradually with more surroundings (39 → 46). **tank_spa** doesn't change, so its confusion with camo and vehicle isn't about surroundings.

**Sites seen in training inflate radar and missile.** On the 58 test scenes that overlap training sites, missile is labelled correctly 82% of the time it is detected, against 53% on the 78 clean scenes. Radar is 87% against 62%. Missile is already at 84% on overlap scenes with only 10 m of surroundings. So the model is recognising objects near-identical to ones it trained on, not using the wider site. Our 136-scene radar and missile numbers are lifted by these scenes; on clean scenes missile recall is 34%, not 47%.

**On bigger patches:** the detector already sees a 307 m tile, and beyond 100 m it gains only about 3 points. For the current detector, larger tiles are unlikely to be the lever. Phase 1 will show whether the surroundings hold information the detector isn't using.
