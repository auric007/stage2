#### Key Observations
- **HR images are sharper:** They contain approximately 5× more fine detail than LR/test images.
- **Higher contrast:** HR images have a much wider contrast range (8–255 vs. 26–215 for test).
- **Different object scale:** HR images have approximately 1.5× smaller pixels per object compared to test images.
- **Domain mismatch:** Feeding the entire `images2x` folder directly into training introduces two distinct image domains.

### 2. **Box size depends on resolution.** Median vehicle box length:

One annotator's boxes grow the same way (7.65 → 8.70 → 10.21 m), so the cause is resolution, not annotator style: at 0.7 m, annotators add about 2–3 pixels per side around a blurred object.
