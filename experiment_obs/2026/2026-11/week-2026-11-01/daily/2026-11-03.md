**Patterns behind the gaps:**

1. **The merge costs ICV and LTV.** Arty has priority over tank and vehicle in the cross-class merge, so arty boxes remove correct ICV and LTV boxes: 92 objects in total. Removing arty from the priority list might recover them, but I haven't tested that. It would also change arty's own TP and FP.
2. **MLRS and EWS are never called by their class.** That is a data or label problem; changing thresholds or the merge won't fix it.
3. **Generic labels are hardest.** Within each parent, the generic labels (RadarSystem, "Camo", ArtillerySystem) and the variants that look like another class (SPA as tank, ICV as vehicle) do worst.
