## New architecture handoff: DINO8 (`/sfs/stage2/models/dino`), read and rescored
- **What arrived** (copy finished ~14:41): a portable bundle of a finished experiment.
  - DINOv3 ViT-H/16+ encoder with an OBB head. Warm-started from the class-agnostic `lr_obb_rgb_v1_best.pt`, then fine-tuned for 8 classes.
  - Trained on native RGB GeoTIFFs: 512 tiles, 14 epochs, focal loss, radar/missile weighted ×2, 16-scene site-disjoint val.
  - Includes 115 cached Test115 NPZ prediction files (quads in native px, objectness, 8-class probabilities).
  - Its data copy `DATA/up42_29oct26` uses the **old class policy**, not the 2026-10-27 rule: test camo 856 / missile 238 / radar 238 vs official 558 / 413 / 275. Scene 23JAN27044234 is missing.
- **Bundle's own numbers.** Best val mAP50 0.722 at epoch 2; the warm start was already at 0.72, so later epochs did not improve it. On Test115 at conf 0.3, the bundle reports:
  - class-agnostic P 61.0 / R 90.6;
  - class-aware MICRO P 45.9 / R 55.4 / F1 50.2.
  - Its `ensemble_comparison.md` baseline is a different pipeline (yoloS2 reranker) on the old labels, so it is not comparable to ours.
- **Our rescore.** Same protocol as HYBRID:
  - setup: `code/score_set.py` on the official 27_10 labels_simple, IoU 0.3, the same 115 scenes; class = argmax, score = objectness × max probability; native px × scale_to_target. Scratch output was not kept.
  - **DINO at its best-F1 conf per class:** P 58.1 / R 50.3, MICRO 53.9 / MACRO 48.7. TP 3404 / FP 2451 / FN 3367.
  - **HYBRID deployed on the same 115 scenes:** P 61.9 / R 66.2, MICRO 65.5 (score_set best-conf) / MACRO 58.6. TP 4482 / FP 2755 / FN 2289.
  - DINO is about even on smallvehicle (62.7 vs 62.1 F1), close on camo (55.1 vs 56.8) and radar (56.5 vs 57.7, at higher P 62.7). It is far behind on vehicle (R 47.1 vs 74.4), arty (F1 31.9 vs 49.2), logistic (14.4 vs 31.5) and missile (41.8 vs 52.7).
  - **Reading.** DINO localizes well (class-agnostic R 90.6) but its class decision is weak. The gap is classification, not detection.
